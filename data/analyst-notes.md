# Base Ecosystem Atlas, analyst notes (draft for owner review, Nov 17 2026)

Every figure below comes from data/base-ecosystem-2026-11-17.csv (30-day window to Nov 17 2026, from DefiLlama) or data/base-funding-rounds-2026-11-17.csv. The Overview reads the numbered list under "Ecosystem insights".

## Ecosystem insights

1. Natives punch above their weight. Base-native projects are 242 of 1,038 rows, about 23%, and hold about 6% of TVL, but take 58% of 30-day DEX volume and about half of revenue. Aerodrome alone is 96% of that native DEX volume.
2. One DEX sets the pace. Aerodrome did $17.4B of DEX volume in 30 days, $8.5M of fees and $6.5M of revenue, more revenue than any other Base project. Uniswap follows with $7.1B and PancakeSwap with $3.1B.
3. Lending is the largest pool of capital. Morpho holds $3.85B of TVL on Base and earned $17.6M of fees in 30 days, the most of any project, while keeping no protocol revenue. Aave holds $469M and Spark $412M.
4. Curated vaults sit on top of lenders. Vault curators such as Steakhouse Financial and Gauntlet report $1.98B of TVL that already sits in Morpho and other lenders, so the Atlas leaves it out of TVL totals.
5. Launchpad fees have cooled. Clanker earned $369K of fees in 30 days, Virtuals Protocol $69K, Zora $3.5K and Flaunch under $500.
6. Consumer AI earns real fees. Venice and Central City each kept more than $1.1M of revenue in 30 days, and Bankr earned $436K of fees.
7. Coinbase money leads Base rounds. Of 89 Base related rounds, the Base Ecosystem Fund appears in 18, Coinbase Ventures in 17 and Base Batches in 12. Since Jan 2025, 50 Base related rounds disclosed about $191M, excluding acquisitions, led by Architect at $35M.
8. Most native leaders raised no disclosed round. Of 108 Base-native projects with a tier, 74 have no round on record, including Aerodrome, which has no priced venture round.
9. Funding research is still thin. 708 of 1,038 projects have not been researched for funding yet, mostly multichain infrastructure and directory listings.
